About the frequency (F) entries: you don’t actually have to enter 1 when a cash flow happens in only one year — the calculator defaults to a frequency of 1. Entering it is just a habit that pays off when a cash flow repeats over several years: instead of keying the same amount multiple times, enter the amount once, then set F to the number of years it occurs.
